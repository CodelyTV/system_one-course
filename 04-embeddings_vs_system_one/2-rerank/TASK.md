# Product Bundles

## Goal

Allow customers to buy a predefined group of products at a discounted price.

## Requirements

- A bundle contains at least 2 active products.
- Each bundle has its own name and a discounted price lower than the sum of its products.
- Customers cannot modify bundle contents.
- Adding a bundle to the cart adds a single line item.
- Bundles are shown in the catalog alongside individual products.
- Seed the database with a few default bundles.

## Out of scope

- Dynamic or customer-configurable bundles.
- Per-customer or time-limited bundle discounts.
